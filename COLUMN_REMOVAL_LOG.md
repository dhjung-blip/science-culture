# Column removal log — canonical analysis files

## 1  Columns removed

| Column | Category assigned in v1 | Populated countries | Populated cells | Reason for removal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `self_efficacy` | science culture (v1) | 18 of 44 | 216 of 528 | Prototype placeholder series; not present in any reported specification |
| `avg_proj_duration` | operational structure (v1) | 18 of 44 | 216 of 528 | Prototype placeholder series; not present in any reported specification |
| `admin_burden_ratio` | operational structure (v1) | 18 of 44 | 216 of 528 | Prototype placeholder series; not present in any reported specification |
| `researcher_retire_age` | operational structure (v1) | 18 of 44 | 216 of 528 | Prototype placeholder series; not present in any reported specification |
| `competitiveness` | alternative outcome (v1) | 18 of 44 | 216 of 528 | Prototype placeholder series; not present in any reported specification |

All five columns were generated by a prototype version of the data-construction pipeline for
constructs that had no harmonized international source. They were dropped from the feature
groups before the analyses reported in the manuscript were run, but the columns themselves
remained in the saved data files. Because they are populated for only 18 of the 44 countries
(the country set of the prototype), leaving them in the released files would present a reader
with undocumented values of unclear origin. They are therefore removed at release.

The 18 countries carrying these values were:

> AUS, AUT, BEL, CAN, CHE, DEU, DNK, FIN, FRA, GBR, ISR, JPN, KOR, NLD, NOR, SGP, SWE, USA

## 2  Confirmation that no reported result depends on the removed columns

- `analysis/` contains no reference to any of the five columns. Every canonical estimation,
  robustness, placebo and figure script in that directory is unaffected.
- `code/config.py` excludes all five from `FEATURE_GROUPS`, so they never entered model training,
  SHAP attribution or the category composites.
- The four category composites recompute bit-identically from the source-derived feature
  definitions after removal: confirmed.
- `code/replicate_paper.py` and `dashboard/scripts/build_data.py` reproduce the earlier v1
  specification and do reference these columns. That specification is explicitly excluded from
  the manuscript (Section 5.4, item 11). If those two paths are retained in the release, they
  should be kept in a clearly labelled legacy directory together with the unmodified v1 files;
  otherwise they should be omitted from the submission package.
